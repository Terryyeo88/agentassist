## Disallowed Categories (§6.1.6, General Guide; §5.13(n), GST Return Guide)

The following are disallowed as input tax claims under Regulations 26 and 27 of
the GST (General) Regulations.  Source wording is reproduced faithfully; square
brackets [ ] mark clarifications drawn from the same IRAS documents.

**§6.1.6 enumerates exactly six categories (items 1–6 below).  Entertainment
of external parties is NOT listed.  Do not treat entertainment as a disallowed
category — see the Entertainment Guidance section below.**

---

### Category 1 — Club Subscriptions / Membership / Transfer Fees

> "Club subscription and membership fees (including transfer fees) charged by
> sporting and recreational clubs"
> — General Guide §6.1.6 item 1
> "Club subscription fees (including transfer fees) charged by sports and
> recreation clubs"
> — GST Return Guide §5.13(n)

**Disallowed:** All subscription fees, membership fees, joining fees, and
transfer fees charged by any sporting or recreational club.

**No exceptions stated in the source documents for this category.**

---

### Category 2 — Staff Medical Expenses

> "Medical expenses incurred by your staff unless —
> a) they are mandatory under the Work Injury Compensation Act or under any
>    collective agreement within the meaning of the Industrial Relations Act;
> b) the medical treatment in respect of expenses incurred on or after 1 Oct 2021
>    is provided in connection with any health risk or requirement arising on
>    account of the nature of the work required of your staff or his work
>    environment; and
>    i.  the medical expenses are incurred pursuant to any written law of
>        Singapore concerning the medical treatment or the provision of a medical
>        facility or medical practitioner; or
>    ii. the medical treatment is related to COVID-19 and the staff undergoes
>        such medical treatment pursuant to any written advisory (including
>        industry circular) issued by, or posted on the website of, the
>        Government or a public authority."
> — General Guide §6.1.6 item 2

**Disallowed by default:** Medical expenses for staff (consultations, dental,
hospitalisation, health screenings, TCM, etc.).

**Allowed (exceptions):**
- Mandatory under the Work Injury Compensation Act (WICA) or a collective
  agreement under the Industrial Relations Act.
- On or after 1 Oct 2021: medical treatment connected with a health risk or
  work-environment requirement, AND either (i) incurred under a written law of
  Singapore on medical treatment / medical facility / practitioner, OR (ii)
  COVID-19 related treatment pursuant to a Government or public authority
  advisory.

---

### Category 3 — Staff Medical and Accident Insurance Premiums

> "Medical and accident insurance premiums incurred for your staff unless the
> insurance or payment of compensation is mandatory under the Work Injury
> Compensation Act or under any collective agreement within the meaning of the
> Industrial Relations Act"
> — General Guide §6.1.6 item 3; GST Return Guide §5.13(n)

**Disallowed by default:** Medical and accident insurance premiums for staff
(group medical insurance, personal accident insurance, etc.).

**Allowed (exception):** Insurance or compensation that is mandatory under
WICA or under a collective agreement under the Industrial Relations Act.

**Note:** This category covers insurance premiums only.  Actual medical
treatment costs for staff are governed by Category 2 above.

---

### Category 4 — Benefits for Family Members / Relatives of Staff

> "Benefits provided to the family members or relatives of your staff"
> — General Guide §6.1.6 item 4; GST Return Guide §5.13(n)

**Disallowed:** Any goods or services provided for the benefit of family members
or relatives of staff.

**No exceptions stated in the source documents for this category.**

---

### Category 5 — Motor Car Costs and Running Expenses

> "Costs and running expenses incurred on motor cars that are either:
> • registered under the business' or individual's name, or
> • hired for business or private use,
> except where the car is excluded from the definition of a 'motor car' in
> Regulation 25(1) of the GST (General) Regulations."
> — General Guide §6.1.6 item 5

The GST Return Guide §5.13(n) makes explicit the following sub-cases:
> "Costs and running expenses incurred on private registered motor cars
>  (including cost incurred for the purchase and running expenses like petrol,
>  maintenance, parking fees) incurred on motor cars unless the car is excluded
>  from the definition of a 'motor car' in Regulation 25(1) …"
> "Expenses incurred on company cars of which the COE has been renewed or
>  extended on or after 1 Apr 1998"
> "Expenses incurred on rental cars hired for use on or after 1 Jul 1999"

**Disallowed:** Purchase cost, lease/hire cost, and all running expenses
(petrol, maintenance, parking fees, road tax, insurance for a private car, etc.)
for:
- Motor cars registered under the business' or individual's name (i.e., S-plate
  / private plate);
- Company cars of which the COE was renewed or extended on or after 1 Apr 1998;
- Rental cars hired for use on or after 1 Jul 1999.

**Allowed (exception):** Cars excluded from the definition of "motor car" in
Regulation 25(1).  Commercial vehicles such as goods vehicles, motorcycles,
and buses that fall outside Reg 25(1) are generally not affected by this
disallowance; however, the specific Reg 25(1) definition should be applied by
the reviewer — this KB slice does not reproduce the full Reg 25(1) text.

---

### Category 6 — Betting, Sweepstakes, Lotteries, Fruit Machines, Games of Chance

> "Any transaction involving betting, sweepstakes, lotteries, fruit machines
> or games of chance."
> — General Guide §6.1.6 item 6; GST Return Guide §5.13(n)

**Disallowed:** Input tax on any such transaction.

**No exceptions stated in the source documents for this category.**

---

## Entertainment Guidance (§6.1.3, General Guide)

**Entertainment is NOT a Reg 26/27 disallowed category.**  It does not appear
in the §6.1.6 enumeration above.  Do NOT surface a line as a Reg 26/27
candidate on the basis of entertainment alone.

### What IRAS actually says about food/drink entertainment

> "a simplified tax invoice for: … (ii) entertainment expenses incurred on food
> and drinks, regardless of the purchase value. You must also keep alternative
> evidence of payment and information on the entertainment details (such as name
> of person entertained, purpose of entertainment, person incurring the expenses,
> etc). This concession applies from 1 Feb 2014 and only on entertainment
> expenses on food and drinks. If the expenses comprise items other than food and
> drinks (e.g. rental of yacht), a full tax invoice is still required …"
> — General Guide §6.1.3(b)(ii)

Food and drink entertainment expenses — whether for internal staff or external
parties — are **claimable** with the appropriate documentation (simplified tax
invoice for food/drink; full tax invoice where non-food items are included).
The Reg 26/27 disallowance rules do not apply to them.

### Reasoning-pass instruction for entertainment lines

**Do NOT surface** a line as a Reg 26/27 candidate because it looks like a
restaurant, catering, or food/drink expense for either internal staff or
external clients.  These are claimable per §6.1.3.

**Surface only when** an entertainment line is bundled with a category that IS
disallowed under §6.1.6 — for example:
- A line that also includes a club membership fee (Category 1).
- A line that clearly shows a benefit provided to a family member of staff
  (Category 4) in an entertainment context.
- Non-food entertainment items (e.g. yacht charter, private jet) where the
  disallowance derives from another §6.1.6 category, not from entertainment
  per se.

When in doubt about a borderline entertainment line, do NOT surface it as a
Reg 26/27 candidate.

## EXCEPTIONS — False-Positive Discipline

The following are commonly confused with disallowed expenses but are generally
**NOT** disallowed under Reg 26/27.  Flag these only with low confidence and
only when additional indicators are present.

1. **Staff welfare (internal meals) and external entertainment (food/drink):**
   Food and beverages for internal staff events and for entertainment of external
   parties are claimable under §6.1.3.  Do NOT flag restaurant or catering
   invoices as Reg 26/27 candidates.

2. **Commercial vehicles:** Lorries, vans, motorcycles, and buses that are
   not "motor cars" within Regulation 25(1) are generally not affected by the
   motor-car disallowance.  Do not flag solely because a line mentions
   "vehicle" — look for S-plate, private car, sedan, SUV, or similar indicators.

3. **WICA-mandatory medical / insurance:** Expenses explicitly described as
   work-injury compensation, WICA-mandated insurance, or injury-at-work
   treatment are claimable.

4. **Work-environment health requirements (post-Oct 2021):** Medical treatment
   connected to occupational health risks and required under Singapore law may
   be claimable; do not flag without other disallowance indicators.

5. **Generic "insurance" lines:** Not all insurance is disallowed.  Only medical
   and accident insurance for staff is disallowed (Category 3).  Fire,
   property, products-liability, and other business-purpose insurance are
   claimable.  Flag only when the description clearly refers to medical/accident
   insurance for staff.

6. **Parking fees for commercial vehicles / public transport:** Parking for a
   non-S-plate commercial vehicle is not a motor-car cost.  Flag only when the
   underlying vehicle is a private car.

7. **Club purchases as ordinary suppliers:** If the supplier happens to be named
   "XYZ Club" but the invoice is for goods or services (e.g., event catering
   billed by a function venue that uses "Club" in its name), this is different
   from a membership subscription fee.  Flag only when the description mentions
   "subscription", "membership", "joining fee", or "transfer fee".
