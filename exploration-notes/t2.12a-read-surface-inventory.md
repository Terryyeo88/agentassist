## Why this note exists

The repo's only saved chain artefact (`chain-run-*.json`) is **compiled output** (F5 boxes +
header-level `fetch_manifest`), not the **line-level reads** the deterministic chain consumes.
Under the move to Excel/CSV extract ingestion, the source adapter must reproduce the chain's
*input*; the faithfulness test is `adapter(extract) == frozen_ground_truth`. Before freezing
anything we must know exactly which live SAP reads a full `review()` performs, the verbatim
field shape each returns, and which fields a realistic client extract can/cannot supply.

This note is the **map**. The follow-on `T2.12a-capture` prompt freezes JSON fixtures against it.

## What a period-only extract CANNOT supply

- **Company-wide DocNum sequence (SEQ_GAP).** S5 issues two *no-date-filter* queries
  (`all_sales_headers`, `all_purch_headers`) specifically to distinguish "DocNum issued in
  another period" from "never issued anywhere". A period-scoped extract is structurally unable
  to reconstruct this — SEQ_GAP degrades to within-period only.
- **`FederalTaxID` (NO_GST_REG).** Sourced from the `BusinessPartners` master entity (S3), not
  from any invoice/credit-note row. A sales/purchase extract alone cannot drive NO_GST_REG; a
  separate supplier-master extract would be required.
- **Line-level `TaxTotal`** (if the export is header-only). Boxes 6/7, NO_GST_REG, and the S4
  SI-line pass all read per-line `TaxTotal`/`LineTotal`. A header/total-only export breaks them.
- **`NumAtCard` (DUP_CLAIM).** Supplier invoice reference; commonly absent from exports.
